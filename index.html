Where do I find my photos?

ONE35 V2: plug the camera in over USB before you develop the roll in the Flashback app, and open the DNG files on it. Developing in the app first clears them off the camera.

ONE35 V1: develop the roll in the Flashback app, then in the gallery choose Share, then Export negatives. Drop the .zip you get here.

Everything happens on this device. Your photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Open Flashnack with start.command

Browsers block this app when index.html is opened straight from a folder. Double-click start.command in the Flashnack folder (or run npm start) and it opens in your browser. Or use the online version.

Drag up or down for exposure, left or right for warmth, right-drag for tint. Arrow keys move between photos. Esc to leave.
